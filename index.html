Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best crypto exchange in the UK?

Kraken is our top pick for UK users in 2026, thanks to its FCA registration, low fees from 0.16%, and free GBP deposits via Faster Payments. For beginners, Coinbase is the easiest to get started with.

Is crypto legal in the UK?

Yes, buying and selling cryptocurrency is perfectly legal in the UK. The FCA regulates crypto asset businesses under the Money Laundering Regulations. You must declare profits to HMRC and pay Capital Gains Tax.

Which crypto exchange has the lowest fees in the UK?

Binance offers the lowest standard trading fees at 0.10%, and Revolut X goes as low as 0.09% for Premium/Metal plan users. Kraken’s maker fee drops to 0.02% for high-volume traders.

Do I need to pay tax on crypto in the UK?

Yes. HMRC treats cryptocurrency as a capital asset. You pay Capital Gains Tax on profits above the annual CGT allowance (£3,000 for 2025/26). Exchanges like Kraken offer transaction history exports to help with HMRC reporting.

Is Kraken available in the UK?

Yes, Kraken is fully available in the UK, is FCA registered, and supports GBP deposits via Faster Payments (instant, free) and CHAPS. It’s one of the most trusted exchanges for UK traders.

What is the safest crypto exchange in the UK?

For maximum safety, choose an FCA-registered exchange such as Kraken, Coinbase, or Gemini. These platforms are legally required to follow anti-money laundering rules and offer 2FA and cold storage for funds.
